
## Cinematic Refrigerator Transitions

Fresh'd uses the refrigerator as its main interactive element.

### Homepage Entrance
- The page begins with a black background.
- The refrigerator fades in with a subtle zoom-in animation.
- The Fresh'd logo and fruit sticker navigation appear.
- The entrance animation plays once when the page loads.

### Navigation Transitions
- Clicking a fruit sticker moves the navigation into a compact top bar.
- The Fresh'd logo becomes smaller in the navigation.
- Each page has a different refrigerator transition.

### Fill Fridge
- The refrigerator zooms in slightly.
- Both French doors animate outward.
- Interior lighting becomes visible.
- The food organization interface appears.

### Customize
- The refrigerator stays closed.
- The view zooms toward the exterior doors.
- Users can decorate the refrigerator with draggable objects.

### Use By
- The refrigerator shifts to one side.
- An inventory form appears beside it.
- Expiration information updates as products are added.

### Buy List
- The refrigerator becomes less prominent.
- A grocery checklist appears in the foreground.

### Returning Home
- The refrigerator closes.
- The original homepage composition returns.
- Navigation stickers spread back into their original positions.

### Accessibility
- Animations respect reduced-motion preferences.
- All pages remain usable without animations.
- Navigation and forms remain keyboard accessible.
